
## 🔧 Step-by-Step Vehicle Assembly Guide

To make our project fully open-source and easily replicable for other students, here is the chronological mechanical build sequence we implemented to construct our autonomous car:

1. **Chassis Baseplate Foundation:** We started by assembling the main structural frame using two long Nezha parallel bars securely connected by two rigid axles, establishing the core length and wheelbase of the vehicle.
2. **Structural Stabilization:** We integrated several intermediate structural parts across the frame to maximize chassis rigidity, eliminate mechanical twisting, and create secure mounting points for the upcoming components.
3. **Central Hub Installation:** In the middle section of the stabilizing platform, we mounted the **Nezha Breakout Module** (which houses the BBC micro:bit) along with the dedicated directional steering motor.
4. **Steering System Assembly:** We built a functional front steering rack geometry and mechanically coupled it to the front directional motor, ensuring precise control over the wheel alignment and turning angles.
5. **Rear Powertrain Framework:** We installed the high-efficiency drive motor on the rear axle, integrating a custom gear train system to transfer the motor's rotational power smoothly to the high-grip rear wheels.
6. **Upper Structure Scaffolding:** We added multiple vertical and horizontal structural supports to act as a protective cage and provide a solid elevated platform for the upper sensors.
7. **Ultrasonic Sensor Array Positioning:** We strategically fixed the three ultrasonic distance sensors onto the frame, orientation-mapping them to cover three distinct fields of view: one facing directly left (J1), one facing directly forward (J2), and one facing directly right (J3).
8. **HuskyLens AI Camera Integration:** Using secure hardware screws, we firmly attached the HuskyLens AI camera to the front section of the protective upper frame, providing the vision sensor with a clear, vibration-free, and unobstructed view of the track.
9. **Cable Management & Safety:** Finally, we routed and tightly bound all connection cables away from the moving parts. This critical step ensures that wires do not get entangled with the rear drivetrain gears or the front steering linkages during high-speed autonomous navigation.
